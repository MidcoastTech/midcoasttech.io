About

Honest tech support, close to home.

Our approach

Midcoast Tech Solutions, LLC provides honest, reliable tech support to homes and businesses across Brunswick and the Midcoast region.

Owned and operated by an United States Air Force veteran with over 10 years of experience in cybersecurity and IT, we provide dependable, honest technology support for small businesses and homeowners across Maine.
From fixing computers and troubleshooting networks to setting up secure Wi-Fi, protecting your business from cyber threats, and managing Microsoft 365, we focus on practical solutions that keep your technology running smoothly. Our experience supporting government contractors, financial organizations, and cloud environments like AWS and Microsoft Azure means we bring enterprise-level knowledge while delivering friendly, local service without the complexity.
